Terms

These terms cover a weekly email report. They do not cover a ranking service, because that is not what this is.

The product

AioPulse checks a fixed list of queries once a week and emails what it saw: whether Google showed an AI Overview, whether your brand was cited, which other names were cited, and the source links shown. Starter is $19.90 per month for 1 brand and 10 queries. Pro is $49.90 per month for up to 3 brands, 25 queries, and a week-over-week diff.

No ranking promise

This is a report, not a ranking guarantee. We do not promise that your brand will be cited, that an overview will appear, or that any ranking will change. Google decides what the overview says. A missing citation is a recorded result, not a failure we owe you a refund for by itself.

Payment and canceling

Payment is handled by Stripe once Payment Links are connected on the site. Until those links are pasted in, the page cannot take money and no subscription exists. After you subscribe, you can cancel anytime. Cancellation stops future reports. It does not rewrite reports already sent. Stripe’s own receipt is where the subscription is managed. We do not store your card on this site.

Your list

You are responsible for the brand names and queries you send. Do not ask us to track a brand you have no right to monitor for a client without that relationship. We may refuse a list that is not a brand-monitoring brief.

Who this is

No company address and no registration number are stated, because this page does not invent one. The offer is the weekly email described above, at the two prices above.
