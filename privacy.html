Privacy

AioPulse is a weekly email report about whether Google’s AI Overview cited a brand on a fixed query list. This page says what that requires. It is not a ranking product.

What we need

  • The email address the report is sent to.
  • The brand names and the queries you ask us to check.
  • The weekly notes we write into that report: whether an overview appeared, whether the brand was cited, other names shown, and the source links on the overview.

Payment

This site does not take card numbers. Once checkout is live, payment is handled by Creem on its own page. Until then, the page cannot take money. We do not publish a company address or a registration number here because none is claimed on this site.

How long

Query lists and report copies are kept so the next email can show a week-over-week diff on the Pro plan, and so we can resend a recent report if you ask. Cancel anytime and we stop the next report.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell the query list.
  • We do not promise a citation, a ranking, or a change in what Google shows.
  • We do not run a public live-monitoring dashboard. The product is the email.

Questions go to the same email thread as the report, once you are a subscriber. There is no separate public contact address on this page.
