Branded AI Overview tracker

See if Google’s AI answer mentions you.

AI Overviews now show up on most branded searches. AioPulse checks a fixed list of your queries every week and emails you whether your brand was cited, who was cited instead, and what changed.

This is a report, not a ranking guarantee. We record what the overview said. We do not promise a citation.

How it works

  1. You subscribe, then reply with one brand name and a fixed query list. Starter holds 10 queries. Pro holds 25, across up to 3 brands.
  2. Once a week we look up that same list and note whether Google showed an AI Overview, whether your brand was named in it, which other names were named, and the source links on the overview.
  3. You get one email. Pro also includes a this-week versus last-week diff.
  4. Cancel anytime. Stopping the subscription stops the next report. There is no ranking work and no promise that a citation will appear.

What a weekly report contains

  • The date the queries were checked.
  • Each query, unchanged from your list.
  • Whether an AI Overview was present.
  • Cited, missing, or replaced, plus the other names shown when your brand was not the one cited.
  • The source links Google showed on that overview, as URLs.
  • On Pro, a short week-over-week note: same, newly cited, or dropped.

The report describes one weekly check. It is not a live dashboard and it is not a ranking guarantee.
